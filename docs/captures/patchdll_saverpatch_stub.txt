=== relocation: stack slots written with bytes derived from the target offset ===
  0x1000335E  mov [esp+0F8h],cl   cl = (offset & 0xFF) + 0x21   (0x10003354/0x10003356)
  0x100033FE  mov [esp+0F9h],dl   dl = (offset + 0x1F) >> 8     (0x100033F4)
  0x1000340E  mov [esp+0F9h],cl   cl = offset >> 8              (0x1000340B, else branch:
                                 chosen when the byte before the aligned
                                 offset is 0xFF or 0xCD, at 0x100033EA/EF)
  0x1000337C  mov [esp+0FEh],al   al = offset >> 16              (0x10003373)

  NOTE: the exact byte offset of these slots *within the stub* is not pinned
  here. The template is copied to [esp+44h] at 0x10003347 and the final rep
  movs reads [esp+40h] at 0x10003455, but further pushes and pops in between
  (the two FindMotif calls at 0x100033A0 / 0x100033CC) shift esp, so mapping
  the slots onto stub offsets needs a full stack-frame simulation of the
  function. Treat the stub offsets as unresolved rather than guessed.

=== 10-byte header written at the blank area before the stub ===

  [4] = dword from [esp+2Ch]   (0x10003444)
  [8] = word  from [esp+30h]   (0x1000344C)
  where [esp+2Ch]/[esp+2Dh]/[esp+2Eh] hold the aligned offset bytes
